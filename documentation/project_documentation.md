\# Project Documentation



\# E-Commerce Business Intelligence Dashboard



\---



\# Objective



The objective of this project was to build a complete Business Intelligence reporting solution capable of analyzing:



\* revenue performance

\* customer behavior

\* operational efficiency

\* delivery performance

\* strategic business opportunities



using SQL and Power BI.



\---



\# Dataset Overview



The project utilized multiple e-commerce datasets including:



\* orders

\* order items

\* customers

\* products

\* sellers

\* reviews

\* payments



These datasets were transformed into analytical business views suitable for reporting and dashboard development.



\---



\# Data Cleaning Process



Data cleaning was primarily performed using SQL.



\## Cleaning Tasks Included



\* handling null values

\* standardizing formats

\* creating analytical views

\* removing inconsistencies

\* engineering customer segments

\* delivery status transformation

\* category label formatting



\---



\# Key SQL Transformations



\## Customer Segmentation



Customers were categorized into:



\* High Value

\* Medium Value

\* Low Value



based on spending behavior.



\---



\## Delivery Classification



Orders were categorized as:



\* On-Time

\* Delayed



based on estimated and actual delivery dates.



\---



\## Category Cleaning



Product category labels were transformed into readable business-friendly names.



\---



\# Power BI Development



\## Data Modeling



Relationships were created between:



\* orders

\* customers

\* products

\* payments

\* reviews

\* sellers



to support cross-filtering and interactive analytics.



\---



\# DAX Measures Created



Key measures included:



\* Total Revenue

\* Total Orders

\* Total Customers

\* Average Review Score

\* Delay Percentage

\* Average Delivery Days

\* Average Shipping Days

\* Segment Revenue

\* Seller Revenue

\# Key Business Findings



\* Revenue concentration exists among a limited number of product categories.

\* Delivery delays negatively impact customer satisfaction.

\* Medium-value customers contribute significantly to overall revenue.

\* Operational delivery performance improved over time.

\* Certain states show consistently higher delivery delays.

\---



\## 2. Cross-Visual Interaction Issues



\### Resolution

\---



\## 3. Technical Identifier Readability



\### Resolution

\---



\## 4. Visual Overcrowding



\### Resolution



Balanced analytical completeness with readability optimization.
